Built on the Traction / EOS framework you already use

Six components. Every workstream below maps to one.

Vision

Facilitated Vision/Traction Organizer session with you and Tom. Lock Core Values, Core Focus (the three-service bundle), 10-Year Target, and — directly — the succession question: build-to-sell, build-to-hand-off, or build-to-hold.

People

Instinctive Drives assessments across you, Tom, and crew leads. Hartman Value Profile benchmarking against your current top performers, so every future hire — toward ~260 people — is screened against what already works, not guessed at.

Data

A weekly Scorecard, AI-integrated with Aspire so it's live, not manually compiled. (Preview on the Dashboard tab.)

Issues

Weekly Level 10 Meetings with a running Issues List — an AI agent sits on the notes and keeps the list current automatically.

Process

Documenting the core 20% of procedures that run 80% of the business — the prerequisite for cloning this model to Spokane, Houston, Dallas, or Salt Lake City. Nothing replicates until it's written down.

Traction

90-day Rocks, owned by you and Tom, with quarterly reviews and weekly check-ins.

Recommended structure

Months 1-3 — introductory$5,000/mo
Month 4 onward — standing rate$10,000/mo

The first 90 days are scoped tightly around the estimating project — the fastest, most measurable proof point — plus the Vision session and initial Scorecard build. The step-up to full rate happens once there are real numbers behind it, not before.

First 90 days

Week 1-2

  • Vision/Traction Organizer session with you and Tom — lock Vision, Core Focus, 10-Year Target, succession framing.
  • ID assessments across the team (you, Tom, sales, top crew leads); Hartman benchmark built from your current top performers.

Week 2-3

  • Launch the Continuity File — a documented, binder-style record of how the business actually runs, department by department, started early so it compounds across the engagement.
  • Begin one-on-one recorded video interviews with your process experts — the raw material for both the Continuity File and every SOP that follows.

Week 3-6

  • Build and ship the AI-assisted estimating workflow against Aspire data — the flagship, fastest-to-prove win.
  • Turn the Week 2-3 interviews into drafted SOPs for Estimating and Routing, your two highest-pain processes.

Week 4-8

  • Stand up the live Scorecard and the weekly Level 10 meeting rhythm with a running Issues List.
  • Continue the interview-to-SOP pipeline through Sales/Quote-to-Close and the Federal 8(a) Bid Process.

Week 8-12

  • Review hard numbers — estimate turnaround, win-rate movement, hours saved — against the introductory fee.
  • First quarterly Rocks review: confirm next priorities and Continuity File chapters, so the 90-day close reads as momentum, not a finish line.
